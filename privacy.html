Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026.

Lambi Videos is a personal application operated by Santino Lambertini for his own use.

Data accessed. When the owner connects his own TikTok or YouTube account, the app receives only the basic profile information and the access permissions needed to upload and schedule his own videos.

Data collected. The app does not collect, sell, share or store personal data about any other person. No third party receives data from this app.

Storage. Access tokens are kept only in the owner's private storage and can be revoked at any time from the TikTok and Google account settings.

Children. The app is not directed to children and has no other users.

Contact. Through the Lambi Videos channels on YouTube and TikTok.
